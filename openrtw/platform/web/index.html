Controls and prototype notes
You control Julii. Fog uses shared Roman sight; the faction filter only changes which visible markers are shown. Unexplored areas stay hidden on the minimap. WASD / arrow keys to move the camera · Shift to move faster · drag to pan · Alt-drag to orbit in 3D · scroll to zoom · left-click a character to see their green movement area · hover to see an action and its route · right-click the ground to move · Shift + right-click to plan without moving · click a town or port to inspect it · Escape clears selection. Continue resumes a planned journey. End turn refills action points. Center on character brings your selection into view. Use the list for characters sharing a cell.
Terrain comes from the original game height map. Julii named characters use the original general mesh and Julii texture candidate. Other armies containing Hastati retain the static soldier prototype; flags mark remaining characters. Terrain uses the complete original height grid, original grass and hills textures, and the original water texture. Sea level, slope selection, lighting and tiling are reconstruction choices. Original terrain blending, walking animation and battle simulation are still unfinished.
